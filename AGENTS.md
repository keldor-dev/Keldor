# Repository guidance

For PowerShell development and refactoring, read the canonical
[PowerShell Engineering Standard](docs/standards/Keldor_PowerShell_Engineering_Standard.md), especially
[function architecture and readability](docs/standards/Keldor_PowerShell_Engineering_Standard.md#function-architecture-and-readability),
and the [Input & Output Standard](docs/standards/Keldor_Input_Output_Standard.md).
Follow [contributor guidance](docs/development/contributing.md) and applicable architecture documents.
Keep detailed standards in those authoritative documents rather than duplicating them here.
